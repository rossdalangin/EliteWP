### Objection: "Can't I just use Elementor?"
**Response:** "Elementor is for hobbyists. It injects thousands of lines of unnecessary code into every page. For a high-ticket agency, you need **Core Engineering**. Our theme is faster, more secure, and ranks better on Google because it respects the W3C standards."
