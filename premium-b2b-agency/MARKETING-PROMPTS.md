## 💡 Pro-Tips for Best Results:
- **Color Consistency:** Always include the hex codes in your 'Negative Prompts' if the AI drifts: `#0F172A` (Obsidian) and `#2563EB` (Indigo).
- **Aspect Ratios:**
  - Use `--ar 16:9` for YouTube/Website headers.
  - Use `--ar 1:1` for Facebook/Instagram posts.
  - Use `--ar 4:5` or `--ar 9:16` for mobile-first ads.
- **Styling:** Keywords like "Glassmorphism," "Matte Black," "Frosted Glass," and "Cinematic Tech Lighting" will keep the AI aligned with the theme's CSS.
