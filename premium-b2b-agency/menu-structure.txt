# ELITE B2B AGENCY - RECOMMENDED MENU STRUCTURE

To maintain high conversion and authority, we recommend the following menu hierarchies:

## 1. PRIMARY NAVIGATION (Header)
The goal is to move prospects from "Curious" to "Committed" with minimum friction.

1. **Home** - The central hub of the acquisition framework.
2. **About** - Establishes agency mission, authority, and team expertise.
3. **Services** - Detailed breakdown of your proprietary B2B frameworks.
4. **Insights** - (Blog) SEO-rich content that demonstrates your market mastery.
5. **Contact** - (CTA) The primary frictionless capture point.

## 2. FOOTER NAVIGATION
Focused on legal compliance, social proof, and secondary deep-dives.

*   **Column 1: Brand**
    *   Home
    *   About Us
*   **Column 2: Systems**
    *   Our Services
    *   Case Studies (Template coming soon)
*   **Column 3: Resources**
    *   Latest Insights
    *   Contact Support
*   **Column 4: Legal**
    *   Privacy Policy
    *   Terms of Service

## 🎯 ELITE TIP:
Keep your Primary Menu limited to 5-6 items max. Use the Customizer to ensure the "Contact" link is always highlighted as the primary Action button.
